[WHAT IS PLINKO CASH?]

Plinko Cash is a casual Android game inspired by the classic Plinko game
famously featured on the American game show "The Price is Right." The concept
is simple yet incredibly addictive: you drop a ball from the top of a triangle-
shaped board filled with pegs. As the ball falls, it randomly bounces left and
right through 20 rows of pegs before finally landing in one of 20 prize slots
at the bottom of the board.

Each slot has a different point value. Slots in the center generally offer the
highest points — up to 1,000 points per ball — while slots at the edges give
smaller rewards. Strategy? There's really no way to predict where the ball will
land, since the physics engine operates naturally and unpredictably. That's
exactly where the excitement lies!
